- The export incorporates Classic baseline NPC data from the [CMaNGOS classic-db contributors](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db), licensed under the [GNU General Public License version 3](GPL-3.0.txt). Classic baseline zones are approximate for WoW: Forever.
- Data from [Efficient Games](https://efficient.games), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The add-on deduplicates NPC names and retains zone labels and observed UI map IDs from the [EG Link 1.60.1.71037 NPC and loot datasets](https://github.com/JIVESCORP/eg-link-output-wowf/tree/main/data/wowf/1.60.1.71037), with observations joined by NPC ID.
